# Registration desk guide

**Goal: no queues.** Each check-in should take about 5 seconds.

## Before doors open
1. Open the link you were sent. If asked, enter the **desk access key**.
2. Tap **Start Scanner** and allow camera access.
3. Check that the badge at the top right says **● Live**.

## For each attendee
1. **Scan** their QR code (phone screen or printout).
2. Look at the big colour banner:
   - **BLUE** → AI track → give a **blue lanyard**
   - **GREEN** → Sustainability track → give a **green lanyard**
   - **ASK** → no track recorded → ask which session they're attending
3. If the button is **green "Check in"**, tap it. Done. Scan the next person.

## Special cases

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| Amber **"Check in anyway"** — *Not in Active Members list* | Their email isn't in the active member list. Follow the lead's policy (e.g. check membership in the PMI app). Tap it only if approved. |
| **Registration cancelled** | Refer to the registration lead. |
| **Already checked in at 08:45** | They (or someone with their QR code) were already checked in. Don't give a second lanyard without checking. |
| **Registration not found** | Type their **name or email** in the search box. If they're still not found, send them to the help desk. |
| **No QR code** | Search by name or email, then tap the result. |
| **Speaker / guest not on the list** | Tap **+ Add guest / speaker**, enter their first and last name, choose the type and lanyard colour, then tap **Check in guest**. |
| Wrong person checked in | Open them (search) → **Undo check-in**. |
| Badge says **Offline · N to sync** | Keep going. Check-ins are saved on the device and sync automatically when the Wi-Fi returns. Don't close the browser tab until it says **● Live** again. |

## Tips
- The **Attendees** tab shows everyone checked in (newest first) and who hasn't arrived yet.
- A handheld barcode scanner also works: click the search box and scan.
- On a phone the result slides up over the camera. Tap **Next attendee** (or the dimmed area) to close it and scan the next person.
